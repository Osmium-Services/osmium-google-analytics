**Google Analytics** loads Google Tag Manager and GA4 on your live site. It respects the visitor's cookie choice, so the **Cookie Consent** service must be installed.

## Set it up

1. In Google Tag Manager, copy your **GTM Container ID**, which looks like GTM-XXXXXXX.
2. Open **Google Analytics Settings**, enter it and tick **Enabled**.
3. Press **Save Settings**.

It only loads on the live site, never on development or staging. It is off by default until your site's design includes it, so ask your developer to confirm.
